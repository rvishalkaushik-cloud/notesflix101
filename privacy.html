Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

This policy explains what Notesflix 101 (the “Site”) collects when you use it, why, and what choices you have. The Site is operated by [Business / operator name], India. Contact: support@notesflix101.com.

1. What we collect

  • Google sign-in: your name, email address and Google account ID, so you can sign in and keep your progress.
  • Study progress: notes you open, XP points, quiz results and the date you joined.
  • XP leaderboard: your first name, class and daily XP. These are shown publicly on the Site.
  • Pass and payments: your plan, its expiry date and the Razorpay payment ID. Card, UPI and bank details are handled only by Razorpay; we never see or store them.
  • Usage analytics: Google Analytics records pages visited, buttons clicked, device type and approximate location, using cookies.

2. Why we use it

  • To sign you in, save your progress and show the XP board.
  • To activate your Pass and handle payment questions or refunds.
  • To understand which notes help students most and improve the Site.
  • To reply when you email us.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

3. Who processes it

  • Google Firebase (sign-in and database) and Google Analytics.
  • Razorpay (payments) and Cloudflare (the server that confirms payments).
  • GitHub Pages (hosting the Site).

These services may store data on servers outside India under their own privacy policies.

4. Students under 18

Many of our users are school students. If you are under 18, please use the Site and buy a Pass with your parent's or guardian's knowledge and consent. A parent or guardian can write to us to see or delete their child's data.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account and progress while your account is in use. Payment records are kept as long as the law requires for accounts and tax. You can ask us to delete your account at any time; payment records we must legally keep are not deleted.

6. Your choices

  • Ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion of your account by emailing us from the address you signed in with.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The Site still works, but analytics will not record your visit.
  • Ask us to hide your name from the XP board.

7. Security

Data is sent over HTTPS, the database only lets each student read their own record, and payments are verified on our server before a Pass is activated. No system is fully secure, so please keep your Google account safe.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

9. Contact

Email support@notesflix101.com for any privacy question or request.
