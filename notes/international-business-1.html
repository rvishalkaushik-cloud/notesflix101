Step 3 · Is the buyer reliable?

A Dubai buyer wants goods worth ₹25 lakh on credit.

Should the Indian exporter immediately accept the order?

✔ Correct answer: B. On credit, the exporter is paid later, so there is a risk of non-payment. Before accepting, the exporter checks the importer's financial position through a bank reference, credit agency report, financial statements and past business record.
🎮 Checkpoint · What would you do?

You are the Export Manager

You work for an Indian textile company. A foreign buyer sends an enquiry for 5,000 shirts worth ₹18 lakh and wants 60 days credit.

What should you do FIRST?

✔ B — Assess the buyer's creditworthiness. Shipping first risks non-payment, and cutting the price or cancelling does not solve the credit-risk question. Check reliability first, then decide on the order.

1. International trade is the exchange of goods and services between two or more countries.

True. That is the standard definition.

2. Before giving goods on credit, an exporter should assess the importer's creditworthiness.

True. It reduces the risk of non-payment.

3. A proforma invoice is generally sent by the importer to the exporter.

False. The exporter sends it to the importer in reply to the enquiry.
