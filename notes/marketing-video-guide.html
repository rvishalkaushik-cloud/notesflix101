PART 1

A great product is not enough

A firm can make an excellent product and still fail if customers do not know about it, cannot reach it, or feel the price is wrong. Marketing connects what a firm makes with what customers actually want.

  • Marketing starts before production and continues after the sale.
  • The customer decides what sells.
PART 2

Marketing and marketing management

Marketing is a social and managerial process by which individuals and groups obtain what they need and want through creating, offering and exchanging products of value with others.

Marketing management is the planning, organising, directing and controlling of marketing activities to achieve organisational goals.

MARKETING MIX · 1

Product

Anything offered to a market to satisfy a need: goods, services or ideas. Around the product itself sit the choices a marketer must make.

BrandingPackagingLabelling
  • Branding: the name, term, sign, symbol or design that identifies a seller's product (brand name, brand mark, trade mark).
  • Packaging: the container or wrapper that protects and presents the product.
  • Labelling: the information tag or printed matter on or with the product.

MARKETING MIX · 4

Promotion

Communicating with customers to inform, persuade and remind them about the product.

AdvertisingPersonal sellingSales promotionPublic relations
  • Advertising: paid, non-personal communication through media.
  • Personal selling: direct oral presentation to one or more buyers.
  • Sales promotion: short-term incentives to encourage purchase.
  • Public relations: building goodwill and a favourable image.
